## 2. Core Contribution

The paper should emphasize three main contributions:

1. **Physics-driven dataset construction**
   - Data are generated by UPPE-based simulations rather than simplified analytical formulas.
   - The dataset includes anti-resonant hollow-core fiber geometry, gas pressure, pulse energy, pulse duration, and nonlinear propagation effects.

2. **Sequence-aware AI surrogate**
   - The Transformer model predicts not only the final output spectrum, but also the propagation-dependent spectral evolution `S(z, lambda)`.
   - The use of z-position and wavelength embeddings allows the model to learn structured propagation dynamics.

3. **Ultraviolet-oriented prediction**
   - The model is evaluated with special attention to the `200-700 nm` ultraviolet band.
   - UV weighted loss and early-z loss can be described as task-specific improvements for dispersive-wave prediction.

## 8. Suggested Short Abstract Revision

Draft:

> Anti-resonant hollow-core fibers provide a flexible platform for gas-based nonlinear frequency conversion and coherent ultraviolet generation. Accurate modeling of ultrashort-pulse propagation in such fibers requires broadband propagation equations with plasma and ionization effects, but high-fidelity UPPE simulations are computationally expensive. Here, we develop a Transformer-based surrogate model trained on UPPE simulations of gas-filled anti-resonant hollow-core fibers. The model takes fiber geometry, gas pressure, and input-pulse parameters as inputs, and predicts broadband spectral evolution and ultraviolet dispersive-wave features over `200-2500 nm`. UV-oriented loss weighting and propagation-distance embeddings are introduced to improve learning of short-wavelength and early-stage dynamics. The proposed AI-assisted framework provides a fast surrogate for parameter scanning and design optimization of fiber-based ultrafast ultraviolet sources.
